FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Kitne din pehle booking karni chahiye?

Weddings ke liye 2-3 mahine pehle, birthdays ke liye 2-3 hafte pehle, aur corporate events ke liye kam az kam 1 mahina pehle booking karwana behtar hai.

Kya aap poore Pakistan me service dete hain?

Ji haan, hum Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan aur Peshawar me services provide karte hain. Baaki cities ke liye bhi travel arrangements available hain.

Advance payment kitni hoti hai?

Booking confirm karne ke liye 30% advance aur event se 3 din pehle 70% baqi amount. Hum installment option bhi provide karte hain long-term bookings ke liye.

Kya customization possible hai?

Bilkul! Har event 100% customizable hai. Apni theme, colors, guest count aur budget ke mutabiq hum package tailor karte hain.

Emergency / last minute booking possible hai?

Ji haan, hum last-minute events bhi handle karte hain depending on availability. WhatsApp pe immediately rabta karein.
